What we fix, install and look after

Sales, service, repair and installation, the same as it says on our van. Call for a free estimate on HVAC repair and installation.

  • Repairs

    Finding leaks, refilling refrigerant, broken fans, compressors and contactor switches. We find what's actually wrong and fix that.

  • Maintenance

    Routine maintenance on your furnace, air handler and outdoor unit, so small problems get caught before the first hot or cold snap.

  • New systems

    Right-sized, energy-efficient installs and replacements. A full install typically takes 3–5 days, and a new system usually lasts 15–25 years.

  • Ductwork

    Install, repair and replace. Ducts start breaking down around 15 years and usually last 20–25, so in an older house they're worth a look.

  • Ductless mini-splits

    Daikin mini-splits for the room the ducts never reached: heating and cooling with a small outdoor unit and no new ductwork.

  • Indoor air quality

    Whole-house air purifiers installed right in your ductwork. See the purifiers we install.

  • Water heaters

    Water heater repair, from the same family-owned company that keeps your heating and cooling running.

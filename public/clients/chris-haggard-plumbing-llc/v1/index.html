Fix the pipe. Keep the yard.

Trenchless repair works from inside the old pipe or underneath the ground, without digging up your property. It’s the same idea as the picture at the top of this page, at sizes from a ¾" service line to culverts you can walk through.

  • Point repair (PipePatch)

    3"–48"

    A patch set inside the pipe, right at the bad spot.

  • CIPP lining

    3"–10"

    A new pipe cured inside the old one, for mains and services.

  • Pipe bursting

    ¾"–6"

    The old pipe is split apart while a new one is pulled in behind it. Mains and services.

  • Horizontal directional boring

    Under it

    New lines bored underneath driveways, roads and lawns instead of trenched across them.

  • Cementitious lining (spin casting)

    30"+

    A new cement wall cast inside large pipes and structures.

Used on storm and sanitary sewer pipelines, catch basins, junction boxes, manholes and culverts, plus erosion control.
